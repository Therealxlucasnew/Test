Imagine this. For four months, everyone around you is lying to your face.

And you have absolutely no idea.

Meet Danish comedian Rasmus Wallbridge.

He thought he was living his dream, making his own movie, a Danish Scary Movie-style parody, with his friend Martin Johannes Larsen.

But the dream was fake. Martin was in on it, with a hidden camera in his lapel pin.

One hundred and forty-nine people were in on the joke. Friends. Colleagues. Even big Danish celebrities.

It was all for a TV two show called Everybody Loves Rasmus.

It premiered on October twenty-sixth, and Denmark exploded.

Comedians and TV hosts called it bullying. One critic called it psychological torture.

Within days, TV two pulled the entire show, and apologized unconditionally, admitting it failed to protect him.

Stars who took part are now saying sorry too. One said he should have known better, and said no.

And Rasmus? He says he's not in a place to join the debate right now.
