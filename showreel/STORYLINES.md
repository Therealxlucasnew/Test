# Showreel: three storylines

Subject: Claude pitching itself as a motion-graphics maker, with a voiceover.
Format: 1920×1080, 30 fps, about 35–45 s, voiceover + music + sound effects.

## A. "Say it, see it" (recommended)

The bold one, and the one a stranger remembers.

- **Device and form:** a sentence whose last word becomes the picture. Every shot ends on the shape that starts the next one.
- **What you see and feel:** the voice speaks and each key word turns into what it means. "Charts" draws a chart, "maps" flies across a map, "3D" lifts the letters into depth. The idea is that you describe something and I build it.
- **First 2 s:** a huge white word, "Describe", fills a hot-orange frame. Its letters snap apart and re-form as "anything".
- **Beats:** hook (say a word, it becomes a thing) → tension (a client brief that's just one line) → reveal ("I'm Claude") → proof (type → chart → map → 3D → glitch, each one a word turned into a picture) → payoff (all of them collide into one frame) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** flat colour fields that change each beat (orange, ink, bone, electric blue), heavy grotesk type, nothing floating in a dark void.
- **Sound:** punchy beat at ~120 BPM, a whoosh or hit on every word-to-picture change, warm male voice.

## B. "Zoom forever"

- **Device and form:** an endless zoom. Every detail you dive into is the next world.
- **What you see and feel:** the camera never stops pushing in. The dot on an "i" becomes a planet, a city light becomes a bar chart, one bar becomes a corridor of type. It feels like falling through a portfolio.
- **First 2 s:** a dense, moving poster of my work. The camera is already diving into one letter.
- **Beats:** hook (the dive starts) → tension ("one line from you…") → reveal (the name inside a zoom) → proof (kinetic type, data, map, 3D, a glitch world) → payoff (pull all the way back to see every world was one picture) → claim → end card.
- **Look:** each world has its own ground colour; one continuous motion-blurred push ties them together.
- **Sound:** a rising synth drone with a hit on each world change, calm voice.

## C. "11:58 pm"

- **Device and form:** a moment in someone's day, told as a chain where each object becomes the next.
- **What you see and feel:** a phone at 11:58 pm and a message: "need a launch video by 9". Claude replies, and the reply bubble unfolds into the video itself, which shows off each style. It ends at 8:59 am with "sent ✓".
- **First 2 s:** a phone lock screen. The clock reads 11:58 and a notification slides in.
- **Beats:** hook (the late message) → tension (a blank timeline, the clock ticking) → reveal (the bubble opens: "I'm Claude") → proof (styles appear as the night goes on, with the clock jumping) → payoff (the finished video plays full-frame) → claim → end card at 8:59.
- **Look:** a dark-blue night that warms to sunrise orange. The phone UI is shown only as a frame.
- **Sound:** lo-fi beat that builds, phone buzz and pop sounds, wry voice.
